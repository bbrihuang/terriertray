# Surface: TerrierTray app (Plan / Week / Menu / Settings)

Mode: Operate. Scope: the whole single-page app. Audience and constraints: see PRODUCT.md.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A dining-hall plate planner organised as a day of meals. The memorable objects are the meal timeline (what's served when, and what you've planned for each meal) and the plate ring (planned vs target). It refuses the health-app reference's assistant, scanning, booking, supplements and invented progress.

OWN-WORLD: Red and white student identity: a warm off-white page, white rounded cards with soft depth, red pills for actions, soft red washes for selection, and near-black ink. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, a two-tone headline, tabular numbers, and authored line icons in round tinted discs.

STORY: A student sees which meal is being served now and what they've planned for each meal of the day. They estimate daily calories with the team's model if they want, build or accept a plate in real portions, check the label and freshness, and walk to the counter. Planned is never presented as eaten.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Brand row, two-tone headline (meal and hall / what to do), a compact hall/day card, the meal timeline card, then the view tabs. On Plan, the plate card with the ring comes first; suggestions follow with one red "Use this plate".

FORM: Standard web controls (selects, list buttons with aria-pressed, segmented tabs, native dialog). The world contributes palette, depth, the two-tone headline, the timeline rows and the plate ring. seed: none (pinned by brief: user's reference image, adapted to red and white).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
