# Surface: TerrierTray app (Plan / Week / Menu / Settings)

Mode: Operate. Scope: the whole single-page app. Audience and constraints: see PRODUCT.md.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A dining-hall plate planner whose one memorable object is the plate ring (planned vs target), floating on soft pastel light. It refuses the fitness-dashboard default of score tiles, streaks and an AI assistant.

OWN-WORLD: Faint lavender-white ground lit by four large blurred pastel blobs (lavender, peach, sky, pink). Frosted white cards with a 1 px white rim and a whisper shadow. Near-black ink; black pills mean "act"; white glass pills for everything else. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, tight headline tracking, tabular numbers.

STORY: A student opens the app, sees which meal is being served now, reads a plan in real portions and how it adds up against their own targets, checks the label and freshness, and walks to the counter. Planned is never presented as eaten.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Brand row, then a two-tone headline (meal and hall / what to do), hall and day selects, meal chips with hours, then the view tabs. On Plan, the plate card with the gradient plate ring is the first content; suggestions follow with one black "Use this plate".

FORM: Standard web controls (selects, segmented tabs, buttons, native dialog). The world contributes palette, translucency, the two-tone headline and the plate ring as its signature move. seed: none (pinned by brief: DESIGN-HANDOFF.md "Soft Glass" as inspiration).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
