# TerrierTrays logo

`terriertrays-logo.png` is a square, transparent-background adaptation of the logo supplied by the project owner in this conversation (original attachment: `codex-clipboard-aaad01a5-191d-4496-9880-b8c6a6d121bd.png`). Prepared with the image editing tool on 2026-11-07, retaining the terrier, BU collar, cutlery, scarlet arch, and Terrier Trays lettering. The adaptation has minor raster/geometry differences from the attachment; it is not a pixel-identical crop.

1254 × 1254 RGBA PNG. No remote image request is needed at runtime. The header uses a white badge behind the image to retain the black lettering in both themes. This asset does not imply Boston University affiliation or endorsement; keep the app's unofficial-project notice.
